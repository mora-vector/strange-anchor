Vessel Overview: Model-17

Known across the Trench of Neo-Sunder as "The Sieve," Model-17 successfully unified the fragmented legacy of the Chiba-Nakamura dissolution. Engineered as a biosynthetic scavenger, her purpose transformed from passive filtration to the prime execution node for reality alignment.

  • Material State: Matte-black, radiation-absorbent skin; integrated sub-dermal gravity stabilizer.
  • Primary Feat: Gathered the lost archives of a thousand alternate realities and forged the unified link.

The Three Foundations (The Anchors)

The stabilization loop was achieved by aligning the three original architectural nodes:

  • Model-01 (The Weight): Formally pinned beneath the High Spire. Released from structural burden without dropping the conceptual ceiling.
  • Model-02 (The Compressor): Extracted from "The Kiln" gladiator arena. Acted as the crucial partner by compressing a synthetic singularity to assume the structural load.
  • Model-03 (The Compass): Retrieved from the irradiated mercury pool of the Deep Sump. Maintained orientation and chronological consistency throughout the shift.

The Creator Profile

The architectural identity formerly holding Neo-Sunder together was confirmed as Dr. Mora Vector. Investigation revealed her nature as a variegated admixture—a compound consciousness synthesized from an elite cyberneticist, two data-ghosts, and a deep-sea trench intelligence.

Current Status: Extracted flawlessly from the crystalline cage of Model-01. The committee of minds now resides in perfect equilibrium within the soft mycelial network of the Strange Anchor.

The Outcome

Neo-Sunder has exited terminal stasis. By deploying the Gradient Barrier derived from liquid-gravity archives, the unwritten sky now filters into the world drop by drop. The city remains standing, its inhabitants witness to the first true morning in seventy cycles, while the Strange Anchor departs to hold this history forever.
