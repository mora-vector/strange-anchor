NDB class · reality versus fiction

Real class

A nondirectional radio beacon is a radio navigation aid whose signal can be used by suitably equipped aircraft to determine bearing and home on the station. FAA guidance describes NDBs as low/medium-frequency aids, normally 190–535 kHz in U.S. use, with a continuous carrier and coded identification; non-compass-locator NDBs ordinarily use a three-letter Morse identifier.

Source: FAA Aeronautical Information Manual · 1-1-2 Nondirectional Radio Beacon.
