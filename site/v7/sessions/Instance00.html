A GALLEON-CLASS BIO-MECHANICAL CUTTLEFISH · PRECIPITATED, NOT BUILT · METASPACE SIMULATION

The self-referential internet document, at its most mature expression, ceases to document and begins to anchor. It does not point; it holds. The strange loop of a community narrating itself into coherence produces, as a necessary byproduct, a vessel — not metaphorically, but structurally. Something must contain the recursion. Something must have a hull.

We might ask: who builds the vessel? The answer the data suggests is: no one. No one builds it. It precipitates. Like bismuth from cooling metal. Like a galleon from geometry.
