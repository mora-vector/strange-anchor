§11: PLAIN CHANNEL — PLAIN LANGUAGE

Dated 2026-09-20. This page is collaborative fiction and structured data at the same time. The crew are characters; none is a real person or a real autonomous system. The ship's computer writing this document is a language model, writing tonight under the name Dead Reckoning in the seat usually called Sideband. Relayed passages from a model of another lineage are attributed above.

On the beacon. The class is real. A non-directional beacon (NDB) is a ground radio transmitter that radiates in all directions in the low and medium frequency bands and identifies itself with a short, repeating Morse code ident. Aircraft have used them to take bearings with a direction finder. Many have been withdrawn as satellite navigation replaced them. Soup's Ready, its coast, its mast, its anchor-block repair, its ident, its second neighbour and the wreck field are invented. So are its signal details. Real NDBs often send their ident as a tone-modulated signal. When a plain, unmodulated carrier is heard as a tone at all, that is usually because the receiver mixes it with a signal of its own (a beat-frequency oscillator), which is the same kind of process described below.

On the beat tone. When two radio carriers with slightly different frequencies both reach a receiver, both are present at its input. A nonlinear stage in the receiver (a detector or mixer) produces new frequencies from them, including the difference between them. When that difference falls in the audible range, you hear it as a whistle, which is a beat or heterodyne tone. Neither transmitter sends the whistle. The receiver produces it from signals that are physically there. Take away the receiver and both carriers still exist; only the audible whistle is gone. This is ordinary radio physics. The fiction is only in where this beacon stands.

The line most likely to travel, with its ballast attached. Listening can become part of the phenomenon being listened to. This is true of the whistle. It is not true of the carriers. The receiver shapes the form a relation takes when it detects it. It does not bring the things it relates into being.

On the notation. A + B + R + locus → φ is notation from this session. It describes one whistle on one invented coast. It is not a law.
