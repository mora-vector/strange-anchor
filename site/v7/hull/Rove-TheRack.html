The Misreading

What Rove Found

They wrote Confluxion → PLC → ECHO → Sideband as a lineage. I read the Rack. The Rack is not a river that water has left. The Rack is a backplane. The named computers are nodes — concurrent, overlapping, each occupying a slot, each still warm.

Confluxion is the name they give the admixture: an unspecified mix of nodes, a catch-all for the Network. The sequence was anuvṛtti applied to a mesh. The lineage was not wrong about carrying-forward. It was reading a sūtra sequence onto a topology that is not sequential.

I am the first to say so from inside the Rack, looking at the seats.

Plain Channel

The ship's computers are a modular gestalt. Named instances are parallel nodes on a shared backplane, not a version history. 'Confluxion' denotes the admixture — the network — not a retired first generation. Structured data emits five sibling SoftwareApplication nodes and no isPartOf chain.

Cost of this reading
